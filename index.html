Hermes Classroom

A personal, single-user Google Classroom integration for deadline reminders.

What it is

Hermes Classroom connects one student's own Google Classroom account to that student's private assistant (a self-hosted Hermes Agent). Once a day it reads the student's classes, their own coursework and submission status, and recent announcements, then sends a short deadline summary to the student's own chat.

What it is not

  • Not a public service. There are no sign-ups and no other users.
  • It never submits, edits, or deletes anything in Google Classroom. Access is read-only.
  • It does not show ads and does not sell or share data.

Permissions requested

  • classroom.courses.readonly — list the student's classes
  • classroom.coursework.me.readonly — the student's own coursework and submission status
  • classroom.announcements.readonly — recent class announcements
